Newell's algorithm own checks (own_checks.adb)

Reference, computed here and not taken from the program:
* plane of a triangle: the cross product of two edges, normalized
  (Newell's summed formula is a different method; the two must be parallel
  and every vertex must lie on the plane);
* bounds: component-wise min and max;
* the five tests of Newell, Newell and Sancha, "A solution to the hidden
  surface problem", Proc. ACM National Conference 1972: Z extents disjoint,
  screen boxes disjoint, P on the far side of Q's plane, Q on the near side
  of P's plane, projections disjoint (own segment-crossing and ray cast);
* after a strict sort, every earlier polygon can be drawn before every
  later one by those tests, and the ids are a permutation. The viewer is
  at large positive Z looking toward -Z, so a smaller Z is farther.

Test data: LCG, default seed 20261008, printed, overridable with AA_SEED.
Mutant split recorded before these checks: seed 20261008, 81 tuning / 82 held-out.

Depth order: a 12x12 z-buffer (larger Z nearer). Painting the sorted list back to front must reproduce that buffer, including after a split of interlocking triangles. The plane check for concave, first-three-collinear and slightly non-planar quads is a fan of triangle cross products, not Newell's sum.
