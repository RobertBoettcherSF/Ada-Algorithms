Sources of every expected value in this folder's tests
======================================================

Nothing is copied from the program's output or from third-party test suites.

tests.adb TEST 1 .. 13 (except 4b): the original checks.

own_checks.adb: see its header (film bins worked by hand from the bin
formula; pixel uniformity by a chi-square bound, df = 9, p = 0.01).

tests.adb TEST 4b (agent A3, 2026-10-09, checker scan of the Is_Valid_*
judges): from the definition of a light path in Metropolis light transport
(Veach and Guibas 1997, path x0 .. xk from the lens to a light source,
interior vertices on scene surfaces): the first vertex is the Camera, the
last is a Light, and every vertex in between is a surface vertex
(Specular, Diffuse or Glossy). All 5 ** 2 + 5 ** 3 + 5 ** 4 = 775 kind
sequences of length 2 .. 4 are judged against that rule; Camera, Camera,
Light and Camera, Light, Light are the hand-picked invalid cases.
