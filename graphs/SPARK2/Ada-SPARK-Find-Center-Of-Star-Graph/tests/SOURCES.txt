Sources of every expected value in this folder's tests
======================================================

Written by the V&V sweep (agent A3, 2026-10-09). Nothing is copied from the
program's output or from third-party test suites.

tests.adb: the original star with centre 3; hand-built stars with centre 16
(as the To end of every edge) and centre 1, and the path 1 - 2 - .. - 16,
which has no vertex on every edge (answer 0).

own_checks.adb (seeded: Park-Miller minimal standard generator, default
seed = FNV-1a (32-bit) of the folder name folded into 1 .. 2**31 - 2,
printed; AA_SEED=<n> overrides it). Reference: the first vertex that is an
endpoint of every edge, checked edge by edge (no degree count), else 0.
* 4,800 stars: every centre 1 .. 16, spokes shuffled and randomly
  oriented, 300 times each;
* 800 near-stars: one spoke replaced by an edge between two other leaves
  (reference 0, checked);
* 5,000 random simple graphs with 15 distinct non-loop edges.
Edge lists with self-loops are outside the problem (README).
