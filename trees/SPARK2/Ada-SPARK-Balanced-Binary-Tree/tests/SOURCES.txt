Where the expected values in tests.adb come from
- the two original cases and the hand cases (chain of 3, chain of 2, empty tree, self-cycle): worked out by
  hand from the definition (at every node the subtree heights differ by at most 1).
- 20,000 random trees with 1 .. 31 nodes (Park-Miller generator, seed 4242): compared with Ref_Height in
  tests.adb, an own recursive height computation written for this test.
- shared children: two hand cases (links 2 / 2; node 4 under nodes 2 and 3) must be refused by Set_Node;
  every left / right link assignment on 1 .. 4 nodes must be refused exactly when a node gets two links
  into it (counted in the test), and every buildable one is judged against Ref_Is_Tree (an own
  visited-set walk) and Ref_Height.
No values were copied from the program output or from third-party sources.
