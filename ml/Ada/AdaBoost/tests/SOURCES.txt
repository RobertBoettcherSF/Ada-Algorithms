AdaBoost own checks (own_checks.adb)

Reference: Yoav Freund and Robert E. Schapire, "A decision-theoretic
generalization of on-line learning and an application to boosting",
Journal of Computer and System Sciences 55 (1997): sample weights start
at 1/n, each round's weak learner is the decision stump of least weighted
error, its weight is (1/2) ln ((1-e)/e), and the sample weights are
multiplied by exp(-alpha y h) and normalized. A stump no better than a
coin toss (error >= 1/2) ends training. Computed here, not taken from the
program's output.

Test data: LCG, default seed 20261008, printed, overridable with AA_SEED.

Mutant split recorded before these checks (seed 20261008): standard family
17 tuning / 17 held-out, so the held-out half is topped up with the alt
family.
