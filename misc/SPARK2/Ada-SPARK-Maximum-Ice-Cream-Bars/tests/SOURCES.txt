Sources of every expected value in this folder's own tests (tests.adb)
=====================================================================

All checks are own properties written for this repository (MIT); nothing
is copied from the program's output or from third-party test suites.

* 3 per bar with 10 coins -> 3, 6 with 10 -> 1, 10 with 0 -> 0, by hand;
  1 per bar with 32,000 coins -> 32 (the shop has 32 bars).
* Own reference: buy one bar at a time while a bar is left and the
  remaining money covers its price; every price 1 .. 1000 with every
  budget 0 .. 3200.
