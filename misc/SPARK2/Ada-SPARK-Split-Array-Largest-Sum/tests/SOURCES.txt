Sources of the tests in this folder
===================================

tests.adb: written with the 2026-10-09 rewrite (V&V sweep, agent B):
the two original cases (7 2 5 10 8 1 3 4 in 2 and 8 parts) plus eight
hand-worked ones (1, 3, 4 and 5 parts; eight 100s in 1, 3, 5 and 8
parts), and the bound floor (log2 800) + 2 on limits tried; the bound
check was committed first (78018057) and fails on the old scan.

own_checks.adb (called at the end of tests.adb; written for this
repository by the V&V sweep, agent B, 2026-10-09). Reference: all 128
cut sets of the 8 elements.
* 3,000 random arrays (half from a range of 6 values), Greedy_Parts at
  40 random limits each against the fewest parts over all cut sets
  (this checks the greedy optimality that the proof does not cover);
* Largest_Sum with every Parts 1 .. 8 on 150 of them and on all-equal
  arrays for 12 values, against the least largest part sum over all
  cut sets with at most Parts - 1 cuts; tries <= floor (log2 800) + 2,
  worst case exactly 10. (Largest_Sum runs its ghost proof loops under
  -gnata, which is slow, so it gets fewer inputs.)
* Random inputs use a Park-Miller generator; the default seed 20261008
  is printed, and AA_SEED=<n> overrides it.
