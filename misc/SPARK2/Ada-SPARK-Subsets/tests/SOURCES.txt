Sources of every expected value in this folder's tests
======================================================

All checks are own properties or own reference code written for this
repository (MIT); nothing is copied from the program's output or from
third-party test suites. Written by the V&V sweep (agent B, 2026-10-09).

tests.adb: the old table (2 ** N for N <= 12) and 2 ** 13 = 8_192,
2 ** 30 = 1_073_741_824; the eight subsets of three items in counting
order with item 1 as the lowest bit, written out by hand, and the wrap;
T T F T F (1 + 2 + 8 = 11) -> F F T T F (12) worked in the comment;
the empty selection; Subset of 10 20 30 (bounds 5 .. 7) for T F T,
F T F, all and none.

own_checks.adb (seeded: Park-Miller minimal standard generator; the
default seed is the FNV-1a (32-bit) hash of the folder name folded into
1 .. 2 ** 31 - 2 (679856080), printed; AA_SEED=<n> overrides it):
* an own recursive generator (leave item K out, then take it) of every
  subset of N <= 10 items; stepping from empty must visit each exactly
  once, with Found False exactly at the last step and the empty
  selection again after it;
* an own rank (sum of 2 ** (K - 1) over the selected K): 5,000 seeded
  random selections of 0 .. 30 items (sparse, dense, top-heavy and
  all-True mixes) must step to rank + 1, or from 2 ** N - 1 wrap to 0;
* Subset on 3,000 seeded random lists of 0 .. 40 items with random first
  index 1 .. 100: against an own recursive extraction, and Subset (S)
  together with Subset (not S) is a rearrangement of the list;
* Count against own repeated doubling for N in 0 .. 30; the ghost Pow2
  table regenerated.
* Any origin (H140, First-relative): stepping a selection of N in 0 .. 8
  items at 'First = 5, 200 and ending at Positive'Last must give the same
  selections (position by position) and Found as at 'First = 1; Subset
  with Items and S at different origins (7 / 1, 1 / 9, 200 / ending at
  Positive'Last) on 300 random lists of length 0 .. 12 (Park-Miller, seed
  20261009) must equal the origin-1 result.
