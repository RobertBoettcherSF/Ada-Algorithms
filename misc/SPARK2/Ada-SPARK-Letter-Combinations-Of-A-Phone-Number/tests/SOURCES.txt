Sources of every expected value in this folder's tests
======================================================

All checks are own properties or own reference code written for this
repository (MIT); nothing is copied from the program's output or from
third-party test suites. Written by the V&V sweep (agent B, 2026-10-09).

tests.adb: the old table's answers as counts of 0, 4 and 12 digits 2
(three letters each: 1, 81, 531_441); all nine spellings of "23" listed
by hand in dictionary order; the four-letter keys 7 and 9; single
spellings decoded by hand in mixed radix (the comment next to each:
"79" K = 5 = 1 * 4 + 1 -> "qx", "27" K = 7 -> "bs", "234" K = 13 ->
"beh"); 2345678 -> 3 ** 6 * 4 = 2_916; the limit: fifteen 7s or 9s spell
4 ** 15 = 1_073_741_824 strings, first, second, fifth and last spelling
written out. Digit_String rejects the digits 0 and 1 and a 16-digit
number.

own_checks.adb (seeded: Park-Miller minimal standard generator; the
default seed is the FNV-1a (32-bit) hash of the folder name folded into
1 .. 2 ** 31 - 2 (1884235203), printed; AA_SEED=<n> overrides it):
* an own keypad written as letter strings and an own recursive
  enumeration of every spelling in dictionary order, compared with Count
  and every Combination (N, K), for every number of 0 .. 3 digits;
* 300 random numbers of 0 .. 15 digits: Count against the product of the
  own key lengths; five random K each: every letter on its key, the own
  left-to-right (Horner) rank of the result equals K, and
  Combination (N, K) < Combination (N, K + 1); the last spelling is the
  last letter of every key;
* the ghost Pow4_Table regenerated (4 ** 0 .. 4 ** 15) and 4 ** 16 above
  Natural'Last; the ghost Suffix_Count and Rank checked against the own
  values by pragma Assert.
