Sources of every expected value in this folder's tests
======================================================

All checks are own properties or own reference code written for this
repository (MIT); nothing is copied from the program's output or from
third-party test suites. Written by the V&V sweep (agent B, 2026-10-09).

tests.adb: the old table's two-colour answers for N = 1 .. 16 (exact
with modulus 2 ** 31 - 1); two colours give 2 * Fib (N + 1), so
T (43) = 2 * 701_408_733 and T (44) = 2_269_806_340, reduced once;
three colours worked by hand (3, 9, 27 - 3 = 24, 2 * (24 + 9) = 66);
one colour (1, 1, then 0); modulus 1; K = 100_000 with modulus
1_000_000_007 worked in the comments (10 ** 10 and
99_999 * 10_000_100_000); K = Positive'Last = 0 mod 2 ** 31 - 1.

own_checks.adb (seeded: Park-Miller minimal standard generator; the
default seed is the FNV-1a (32-bit) hash of the folder name folded into
1 .. 2 ** 31 - 2 (596083936), printed; AA_SEED=<n> overrides it):
* brute force over every colouring of N posts with K <= 6 colours
  (K ** N <= 2 ** 16), counting those without three equal neighbours;
* the run formula: a colouring is a sequence of B runs of length 1 or 2,
  placed in C (B, N - B) ways and coloured in K (K - 1) ** (B - 1) ways.
  The sum over B, mod M, with Pascal's triangle mod M, on 30 seeded
  random (N <= 1_000, K, M), small and large;
* Count (N, K, M) = Count (N, K + M, M).
