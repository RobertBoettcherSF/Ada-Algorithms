Sources of the tests in this folder
===================================

tests.adb: written with the 2026-10-09 rewrite (V&V sweep, agent B):
a hand-worked mountain (1 3 7 12 10 6 2 0 ...), a skewed mountain
(up by 1, down by 3) for every peak position 2 .. 31, and the bound
floor (log2 N) + 2 on comparisons; the bound check was committed first
(b1033a8e) and fails on the old linear arg-max.

own_checks.adb (called at the end of tests.adb; written for this
repository by the V&V sweep, agent B, 2026-10-09). Reference: the index
of the largest element (linear arg-max); for membership, a direct
existential test (some P with a strict rise up to P and a strict fall
after it), independent of the no-valley form of the predicate.
* for every peak position, a mountain with steps of 1 and 50 with
  random steps; 20,000 random mountains: peak, comparisons
  <= floor (log2 N) + 2, worst case exactly 5;
* Mountain_Array membership against the existential test on 20,000
  mountains with one element changed by -6 .. 6 (both outcomes occur
  more than 1,000 times).
* Random inputs use a Park-Miller generator; the default seed 20261008
  is printed, and AA_SEED=<n> overrides it.
