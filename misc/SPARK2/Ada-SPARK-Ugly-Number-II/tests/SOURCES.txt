Sources of every expected value in this folder's tests
======================================================

All checks are own properties or own reference code written for this
repository (MIT); nothing is copied from the program's output or from
third-party test suites. Written by the V&V sweep (agent B, 2026-10-09).

tests.adb:
* the ugly numbers up to 36 by hand (1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 8, 9, 10, 12, 15,
  16, 18, 20, 24, 25, 27, 30, 32, 36; 7, 11, 13, 14, 17, 19, 21, 22, 23,
  26, 28, 29, 31, 33, 34, 35 have another prime factor), then 40, 45, 48,
  50, 54, 60 as the 21st .. 26th (37 .. 60 by hand);
* the exponents of 1 = (0, 0, 0), 30 = 2 * 3 * 5 = (1, 1, 1) and
  36 = 2 ** 2 * 3 ** 2 = (2, 2, 0);
* the limit values, each counted by the own enumeration in own_checks.adb
  (every 2 ** a * 3 ** b * 5 ** c up to 8_062_156_800, sorted) and
  factored by hand: the 1_691st is 2_125_764_000 = 2 ** 5 * 3 ** 12 *
  5 ** 3 (the last that fits a 32-bit Positive), the 1_692nd is 2 ** 31 =
  2_147_483_648, and the 2_000th is 8_062_156_800 = 2 ** 14 * 3 ** 9 *
  5 ** 2;
* own property for First_Ugly (Max_N): increasing, starts at 1, every
  entry free of other prime factors (own trial division by 2, 3, 5).

own_checks.adb (seeded: Park-Miller minimal standard generator; the
default seed is the FNV-1a (32-bit) hash of the folder name folded into
1 .. 2 ** 31 - 2 (1489547457), printed; AA_SEED=<n> overrides it):
* an own enumeration of every 2 ** a * 3 ** b * 5 ** c up to
  8_062_156_800 (three nested loops), insertion-sorted: at least 2_000
  values, and the 1_691st, 1_692nd and 2_000th are the values above;
* First_Ugly (2_000) against it entry by entry, and the exponents of every
  entry against an own factorization (count the factors 2, 3 and 5; trial
  division from 7 finds no other factor);
* First_Ugly (N) for every N <= 60, Nth_Ugly at 40 seeded N <= 600, and
  the exponents of the last entry at 20 seeded N <= 400;
* Lemma_Complete run (assertions on, so the ghost code executes) for every
  exponent triple whose value is at most the 300th ugly number, and the
  number of such triples is exactly 300 (the K-th ugly number has exactly
  K ugly numbers up to it).
Run: gnatmake -gnata -gnatwa -gnat2022 own_checks.adb && ./own_checks
(4,124 checks, about 6 s with assertions on; without -gnata a
planted "I3 advances only when 2 * L (I2) is not also the minimum" (so 6
is listed twice) fails 4,101 of them, and a planted "the 3-branch does not
raise the exponent of 3" fails 1,759).
