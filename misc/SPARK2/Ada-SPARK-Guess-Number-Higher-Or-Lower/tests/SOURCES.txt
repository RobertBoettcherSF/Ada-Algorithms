Sources of the tests in this folder
===================================

tests.adb: written with the 2026-10-09 rewrite (V&V sweep, agent B):
five games with their guess sequences worked out in the comments.

own_checks.adb (called at the end of tests.adb; written for this
repository by the V&V sweep, agent B, 2026-10-09). There is no second
search to compare with; the checks are properties over every game:
* all 528 games (N in 1 .. 32, Secret in 1 .. N): the answer is Secret;
  the guess count is between 1 and M (N), the smallest M with
  2 ** M - 1 >= N (computed by doubling); some secret needs exactly M (N)
  guesses; at most 2 ** (K - 1) secrets need exactly K guesses;
* Probe against the integer order for all 32 x 32 pairs.
* No randomness, so no seed.
