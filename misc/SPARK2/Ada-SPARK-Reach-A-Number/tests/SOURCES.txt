Sources of every expected value in this folder's tests
======================================================

Written by the V&V sweep (agent A3, 2026-10-09). Nothing is copied from the
program's output or from third-party test suites.

tests.adb: the original values (0, 2, 3, -4, 100); hand-worked: 1 and -1 in
one move; 5 in five moves (1+2+3 and 1+..+4 overshoot by an odd amount,
1+..+5 = 15 overshoots by 10 = 2 * 5: 1+2+3+4-5); 6 = 1+2+3; -100 mirrors
100.

own_checks.adb: exhaustive over Value -100 .. 100 against a reachable-set
search (the positions reachable after K moves are built move by move in a
Boolean table; first K that holds Value), no parity formula.
