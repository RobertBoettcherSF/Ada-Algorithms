Sources of every expected value in this folder's tests
======================================================

Written by the V&V sweep (agent A3, 2026-10-09). Nothing is copied from the
program's output or from third-party test suites.

tests.adb: the first line of the main program is the original case. Check_Edges
(hand-worked from the deque definition, Capacity = 8 from the spec):
eight Push_Front of 1 .. 8 fill the deque (Is_Full only at 8) and come back
1 .. 8 from Pop_Back; a ninth push at either end and a pop at either end of
an empty deque break the preconditions and must raise Assertion_Error
(tests run with -gnata); eight Push_Back of 10 .. 80 come back in the same
order from Pop_Front; an interleaved sequence whose contents are traced in
the comments (2 1 3 -> pop back 3 -> 6 2 1 4 5 -> pops 6, 2, 5, 4, 1)
crosses the ends of the internal array; a single element is both front and
back (Integer'First, Integer'Last); Initialize empties a non-empty deque.

own_checks.adb: model-based. 300 seeded random runs of 200 steps apply the
same operations to the deque and to a plain array kept in front-to-back
order (shifted on front operations, no ring buffer); Length, Is_Empty and
Is_Full are compared after every step and every pop must return the
model's front / back value. Seed = FNV-1a of the folder name, AA_SEED
overrides it.
