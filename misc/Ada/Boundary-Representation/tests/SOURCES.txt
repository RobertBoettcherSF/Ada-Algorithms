Sources of every expected value in this folder's tests
======================================================

Nothing is copied from the program's output or from third-party test suites.

tests.adb TEST 1 .. 13: the original checks; expected counts follow from
the operations applied (each Make_* / Euler operator adds or removes a
known number of vertices, edges and faces) and from the Euler-Poincare
formula V - E + F (Euler 1758; Poincare 1895; Mantyla, An Introduction
to Solid Modeling, 1988, for the Euler operators). 5.5 (changed by agent
A3, 2026-10-09): a one-vertex MVFS skeleton has V - E + F = 2 but no
edges, so it is not a closed surface.

tests.adb TEST 14 (agent A3, 2026-10-09, checker scan of the Is_Valid_*
judges): from the definition of a closed orientable 2-manifold polygon
mesh (every edge on exactly two faces that traverse it in opposite
directions; the faces around every vertex form one ring) and
V - E + F = 2 - 2g per connected shell (Mantyla 1988, ch. 2-3). Meshes
built by hand: a cube (8 vertices, outward loops), a 3 x 3 quad torus
(9 vertices, 18 edges, 9 faces, V - E + F = 0, genus 1), the cube with
one face reversed (orientation clash, still V - E + F = 2), two disjoint
cubes (two genus-0 shells, V - E + F = 4), a tetrahedron, and the
tetrahedron with one dangling edge (V - E + F = 2, edge on no face).

tests.adb TEST 15 (agent A3): the same hand-built meshes with the
expected Check_Solid failure kind, shell count and genus (torus genus 1;
cube + torus genus 0 then 1); two tetrahedra glued at one vertex (every
edge fine, two rings at the shared vertex); an extra isolated vertex; a
tetrahedron missing a face; the empty model; the tetrahedron again via
Make_Polygon_Face (6 shared edges) and a repeated-vertex loop.

tests.adb TEST 16 (agent A3): an own reference written in the test from
the same definition but on vertex indices, not edge records: directed
edge counts (each edge unused or used once each way), every vertex used,
the link of each vertex one directed cycle, shells as vertex components
through triangles, genus from V - E + F per shell. Compared with
Check_Solid on all 59,049 meshes of 5 vertices (each of the 10 triangles
absent or in one of two orientations); 20 are closed manifolds (the 10
triangular bipyramids, each in both orientations).
