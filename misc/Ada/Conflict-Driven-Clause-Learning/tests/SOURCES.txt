Sources of the expected values in own_checks.adb (own code, MIT)
================================================================
* SAT / UNSAT: brute force over all 2**N assignments (bit masks), written
  for this repository; it shares no code with cdcl.adb.
  - 200 random 3-SAT formulas, 8 .. 11 variables, three distinct variables
    per clause, M = 4.26 N (odd rounds) or 5.2 N (even rounds), so SAT and
    UNSAT both occur often; SAT and UNSAT agreement are reported separately.
  - 200 easy formulas: 1 .. 6 variables, 0 .. 8 clauses of width 1 .. 4,
    duplicate and complementary literals allowed; reported separately.
  - Pigeonhole PHP(3,2) and PHP(4,3) (standard textbook encoding: each pigeon
    in some hole, no two pigeons in one hole), UNSAT.
* Certificates: every SAT answer's assignment is evaluated on every clause
  by own_checks' own evaluator (unassigned counts as neither value). Beyond
  brute force: 12 formulas with 30 .. 120 variables and 4 N clauses built
  around a hidden assignment (clauses it falsifies get a literal flipped),
  so UNSAT is always wrong there.
* All three solver variants (Basic, Restarts with interval 1 .. 6, Clause
  deletion with Max_Learned 1 .. 6) on every formula. Park-Miller PRNG,
  seed 20261008.
* tests.adb: exit status is now nonzero when a check fails (the final
  pragma Assert is ignored without -gnata).
Nothing is taken from the program's output or from third-party test suites.
