RIPEMD-160 own checks (own_checks.adb)

The expected digests are the test vectors published by the original authors
on https://homes.esat.kuleuven.be/~bosselae/ripemd160.html (Antoon Bosselaers,
KU Leuven), the same values as Appendix B of H. Dobbertin, A. Bosselaers and
B. Preneel, "RIPEMD-160: A Strengthened Version of RIPEMD" (18 April 1996,
the authors' corrected version of the FSE 1996 paper), hosted at
https://homes.esat.kuleuven.be/~bosselae/ripemd160/pdf/AB-9601/AB-9601.pdf.
Copied into tests/authors_vectors.txt. No implementation was copied.

Each vector is hashed in one shot and again in chunks (sizes 1, 55, 56, 63,
64 and sizes drawn from an LCG). The chunked result is compared with the
published digest, not with the one-shot result. The LCG seed defaults to
20261008, is printed, and is overridable with AA_SEED.

Mutant split (before these checks were written): seed 20261008, standard
family 34 tuning / 27 held-out.
