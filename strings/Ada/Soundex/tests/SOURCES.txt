Sources of every expected value in this folder's tests
======================================================

Nothing is copied from the program's output or from third-party test suites.

tests.adb sections 1 .. 15: the original checks; expected codes follow
from the American Soundex rules stated in soundex.ads (letter-to-digit
map, H/W and vowel separation, '0' padding).

tests.adb Is_Valid_Code and section 16 (agent A3, 2026-10-09, checker scan
of the Is_Valid_* judges): from the Code_String definition in soundex.ads
(an uppercase letter, then digits; letters coded 0 are dropped, so every
produced digit is 1 .. 6, and '0' appears only as right padding). All
18,278 words of 1 .. 3 letters must give such a code; five hand-made
codes check the judge itself.
