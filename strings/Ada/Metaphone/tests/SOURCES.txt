Sources of every expected value in this folder's tests
======================================================

Nothing is copied from the program's output or from third-party test suites.

tests.adb sections 1 .. 16: the original checks; expected codes follow
from the rules stated in metaphone.ads (original Metaphone, Philips 1990,
as in the Apache Commons Codec description).

tests.adb Is_Valid_Code and section 17 (agent A3, 2026-10-09, checker scan
of the Is_Valid_* judges): from the code alphabet stated in metaphone.ads
("0 B F H J K L M N P R S T W X Y, and leading vowels"; a one-letter word
encodes to itself, step 2). All 18,278 words of 1 .. 3 letters must
either raise Invalid_Argument (empty code, as the spec states) or give a
code in that alphabet; five hand-made codes check the judge itself.
