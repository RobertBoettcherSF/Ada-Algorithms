Sources of every expected value in this folder's tests
======================================================

Written by the V&V sweep (agent A3, 2026-10-09). Nothing is copied from the
program's output or from third-party test suites.

tests.adb: the first two asserts are the original cases. Hand-worked: one
point gives Long_Long_Integer'Last (no pair); (3, 0)-(5, 0) = 4 and
(0, -7)-(0, -4) = 9; (-1000, -1000)-(1000, 1000) = 2000 ** 2 * 2 =
8_000_000; (100, 100), (-100, 50), (300, -20), (101, 99): the closest pair
uses the last point, 1 + 1 = 2, and without it 200 ** 2 + 50 ** 2 = 42_500;
repeated (0, 0) points give 0.

own_checks.adb: 60,000 seeded random point sets (seed = FNV-1a of the
folder name, AA_SEED overrides it), 1 .. 16 points on squares of side 3,
20, 300 or 2001 (small squares force ties and repeats). Reference: a
different algorithm, the X-sorted strip sweep (insertion sort by X, then
for each point only later points whose X gap squared is below the best so
far). Properties: the same answer for the points in reverse order and
shifted to touch -1000; Distance_Squared against the coordinate formula.
