Sources of every expected value in this folder's tests
======================================================

Nothing is copied from the program's output or from third-party test suites.

tests.adb TEST 1 .. 14: the original checks. The precedence matrix for
S -> aSb | c and the expected parse results follow from the Wirth-Weber
relations of that grammar, derived by hand.

tests.adb TEST 15 (agent A3, 2026-10-09, checker scan of the Is_Valid_*
judges): from the definition of a simple precedence grammar (Wirth and
Weber, "EULER: a generalization of ALGOL", CACM 1966; Aho and Ullman, The
Theory of Parsing, Translation and Compiling, vol. 1, 1972): no empty
rule, no two rules with the same right-hand side, and at most one of the
relations X = Y (X Y adjacent in a rule), X < Y (X B adjacent, Y in
FIRST+(B)), X > Y (A W adjacent, X in LAST+(A), Y = W or Y in FIRST+(W))
for every ordered pair. The relations of the four grammars are derived
by hand: left recursion gives a = B and a < B; right recursion gives
A = e and A > e; S -> aAb, A -> d has no clash; '$' is reserved as the
end marker.
