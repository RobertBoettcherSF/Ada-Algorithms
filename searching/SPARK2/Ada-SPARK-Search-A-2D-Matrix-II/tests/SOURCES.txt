Sources of the tests in this folder
===================================

tests.adb: written with the 2026-10-09 rewrite (V&V sweep, agent B):
three row- and column-sorted 8 x 8 matrices (1 .. 64 row by row;
4 R + 3 C, whose rows overlap; R + C, equal along anti-diagonals), every
target 0 .. 100 against a scan of every entry, and the bound
Rows + Cols - 1 = 15 on comparisons (the length of a monotone path from
the top-right to the bottom-left corner, counted step by step), which
must also be reached. The bound check was committed first and fails on
the old full scan (64 comparisons). The original test (two pragma
Asserts on 1 .. 64) is kept as part of the first matrix.

own_checks.adb (called at the end of tests.adb; written for this
repository by the V&V sweep, agent B, 2026-10-09). Reference: a scan
over every row and column of the 2D matrix (it does not use Cell); for
the predicate, every entry <= its right neighbour and <= the entry
below, checked on (R, C) directly.
* Position_Of and Cell against the entries of a numbered matrix;
* 5 fixed (the three above, 10 C + R, all 50) and 300 random sorted
  matrices (each entry = the larger of its upper and left neighbours
  plus 0 .. k, k = 1 .. 6), each with every target 0 .. 100: answer, at
  least one comparison, exactly one when Target is the top-right entry,
  measured worst case exactly 15; exact counts below every entry (8,
  along row 1) and above every entry (8, down column 8);
* exact counts on 1 .. 64 for every target: (R - 1) + (Cols - C) + 1
  for the cell (R, C) that holds it;
* Staircase_Matrix membership on each with one entry lowered below its
  left or upper neighbour (always rejected), and on 2,000 random
  matrices (half of them sorted with one entry changed).
* Random inputs use a Park-Miller generator; the default seed 20261009
  is printed, and AA_SEED=<n> overrides it.
