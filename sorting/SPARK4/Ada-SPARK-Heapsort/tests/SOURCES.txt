Sources of every expected value in this folder's tests
======================================================

Nothing is copied from the program's output or from third-party test suites.

tests.adb, original sections: expected outputs are the inputs in
ascending order, computed by the test's own Reference_Sort (an insertion
sort written in the test) or written by hand for small inputs.
Permutation is judged by Is_Permutation (sort both copies with
Reference_Sort, compare element by element).

Section "Is_Perm (Post) against sorted copies" (agent A3, 2026-10-09,
permutation Post): every pair of arrays of length 0 .. 4 over -1 .. 1 at
origins 1 and 7 (14,762 pairs; 2 x sum of 9^Len). The Post predicate
Is_Perm (counts per value) must equal Is_Permutation (sorted copies) on
every pair. Two hand cases: an all-zeros result and equal value sets with
different counts must be rejected.
