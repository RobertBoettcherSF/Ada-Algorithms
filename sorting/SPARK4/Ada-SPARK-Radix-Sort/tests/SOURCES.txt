Sources of the expected values in own_checks.adb
================================================

* Own stable reference: insertion sort by one base-16 digit (By_Digit in
  own_checks.adb), written for this repository.
* LSD radix sort definition (stable digit passes from the least
  significant digit): Wikipedia "Radix sort", and Knuth TAOCP vol. 3,
  5.2.5. Two passes for 8-bit keys in base 16.
* Hand cases: 16#21#, 16#31# (equal low digit) keep input order in pass 1;
  16#22#, 16#21# (equal high digit) come out in pass-1 order;
  16#12#, 16#21# are out of order after pass 1 alone.
