Sources of every expected value in this folder's own tests (tests/main.adb)
==========================================================================

All checks are own properties or own reference code written for this
repository (MIT); nothing is copied from the program's output or from
third-party test suites. Random inputs come from the Park-Miller minimal
standard generator (seed 20261008).

* 2 1 3 5 6 4 7 -> 2 3 6 7 1 5 4 (positions 1 3 5 7, then 2 4 6), by hand.
* Own reference (the standard Odd Even Linked List statement: group the
  nodes by odd and even position, not by value): every other position
  starting at the first, then every other position starting at the
  second, each in original order. The length must not change.
* Inputs: every list of length 0 .. 7 over {0, 1, 2} (exhaustive) and
  3,000 random lists of length 0 .. 16 (every fourth full) over -20 .. 20.
