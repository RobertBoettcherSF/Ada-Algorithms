Wavelet own checks (own_checks.adb)

Float Haar, non-normalized, written here:
  average (x+y)/2, detail (x-y)/2, packed low then high.
  Inverse: x = a+d, y = a-d.
  This is the averaging/differencing form used since Haar (1910), not an
  orthonormal scaling by 1/sqrt(2). A 2D level is that 1D step on each row,
  then on each column.

Integer form: the lifting S-transform of Calderbank, Daubechies and
Sweldens, "Wavelet Transforms That Map Integers to Integers", Applied and
Computational Harmonic Analysis 5 (1998), 332-369, written so the sum is
never formed:
  d = y - x,  s = x + floor(d / 2)
  inverse: x = s - floor(d / 2),  y = x + d
This equals floor((x+y)/2) when the sum fits. Floor is toward -infinity.
An even value, including the most negative one, divides evenly; an odd
negative is one below Ada's truncating division. Coefficients are Detail, a distinct 64-bit integer. Integer samples
are converted one at a time (Detail (B) - Detail (A)); the difference
of Integer'Last and Integer'First is -(2**32-1), which is not an
Integer subtraction. The average is converted back to Integer. A
further level can double a detail again (2**31 is not an Integer).
Low coefficients, then details. Further levels repeat the step on the
low-pass prefix only.
